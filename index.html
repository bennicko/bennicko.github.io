Reading

I'm a big fan of reading, both fiction and non-fiction. I usually try to alternate between the classes one at a time.

  • The Chaos Machine: The Inside Story of How Social Media Rewired Our Minds and Our World

    This book is an absolute must read in modern day. It holds your hand and walks you through how Big Tech companies became obsessed with optimising their algorithms for engagement, and the damage this caused (including, but not limited to: A genocide in Myanmar, anti-vax misinformation during 2020, and shockingly, the attack on the Capitol building on Jan. 6th). Big Tech companies like YouTube and Meta have practically discovered mind control from first principles. I encourage everyone to read this for themselves - it's impossible to look at the world the same away again after completing this book, in a good way. It's a modern requirement for self-defense.

  • 1984

    I don't think you need me to tell you about 1984. It speaks for itself. Thoroughly enjoyed it, and am endlessly fascinated that, no matter where people land on the political spectrum, everyone assumes Orwell is on their side. A testament to his unique style of writing.
